# Aspire — Helping Students Rise

A volunteer-led, corporate-community initiative that directly funds education for students from low-income families.

## About
Founded by **Shashikala Subramani** on 12 May 2026, Aspire connects like-minded corporate professionals with underprivileged students — paying fees directly to institutions with zero overhead and zero middlemen.

Every student supported by Aspire is encouraged to give back when they graduate, continuing the legacy.
